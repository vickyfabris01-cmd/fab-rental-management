Differences from the agreed plan, to settle before building

  • Subscription page offers "M-Pesa" and "Card". The plan is M-Pesa only for now.
  • Business cards, the business page and the product page show star ratings and a Reviews tab. Reviews are not in the plan and will be left out.
  • Onboarding shows prices as "per month" for each type. Pricing and billing period are set by the super admin, so these are placeholders.
  • System settings (sheet 11) show a "Default commission (%)" field. The plan charges a subscription, not a commission. The field will be stored and shown, but nothing is charged from it until you decide.
  • Messaging (sheet 8) shows one-to-one chat. It was not in the plan. It is scheduled in Phase 3 as simple message threads between a resident or worker and the manager.
  • Sheet 7 uses equipment examples (pickup truck, power tiller, generator). For rental, assets become rooms and bookings become rental requests and tenancies.
  • Sheet 11 shows maintenance mode and "2FA required" switches. Maintenance mode is built. Two-factor sign-in is not planned; the switch will be hidden until it is.
  • The dark desktop Properties screen on sheet 5 is titled "Residents". A labelling slip to fix in the final image.

Notes on the numbering

  • Two sheets are labelled 06/10. They are kept here as 6A and 6B. The super admin sheet is labelled 11/10.
  • Sheet 6B mixes resident screens with manager tables (rooms, complaints, payroll). Those manager tables are built in Phase 3 beside the resident screens they match.
  • Sheet 10 has two sections numbered 9 (both Support and Help).
